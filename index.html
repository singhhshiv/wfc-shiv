Cafe Life

Two years of working from the best cafes in India

For the past two years, a cafe has been my office. Not because I had to, but because the right cafe does something to the way you think. The ambient noise, the smell of coffee, a table by the window. Some of my best work has happened in places like these.

I'd love to connect with people who work from cafes too, baristas, owners, regulars, founders, creators. Especially in NCR, Udaipur, and Jaipur. If you work at or run a place I'd love to know about, say hello.
