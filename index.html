How it works

Building something real takes more than hustle.

The path from idea to company isn't linear. EO members range from pre-idea to pre-launch, but all share one thing: the conviction to build something that lasts. We support builders at two stages:

01 — Membership

Core Membership

6 months · No cost or equity · Explore & Build

A curated, high-trust community of founders, operators, and domain experts — testing ideas, finding co-founders, and building conviction on what to pursue and how.

  • Weekly deep dives, events, and workshops
  • Peer network of 500+ active members worldwide
  • Direct intros to EO portfolio and alumni companies
  • Mentorship from founders who've scaled before
  • No strings — no cost, no equity, no commitment
Apply for Membership

02 — Fellowship

Founder Fellowship

$500K funding · 6% equity · Conviction & Launch

Capital and community for founders who are ready to build. We back you early — before product-market fit — so you can move with speed and conviction rather than desperation.

  • $300K upfront for 6% + $200K in your next round
  • Dedicated partner support multiple times per week
  • Demo Day access and bespoke investor introductions
  • Up to $500K in cloud and tooling credits
  • Flexible timelines — no artificial sprint deadlines
