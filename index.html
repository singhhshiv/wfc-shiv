Café Life

Two years of working
from the best cafés in India

For the past two years, a café has been my office. Not because I had to — but because the right café does something to the way you think. The ambient noise, the smell of coffee, the unhurried pace of a table by the window. Some of my best work has happened in places like these.

I'd love to connect with people who work from cafés too — baristas, owners, regulars, founders, creators. Especially in NCR, Udaipur, and Jaipur. If you work at or run a café I'd love in this list, say hello.
